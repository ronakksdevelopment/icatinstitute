Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Find quick answers to the most common queries about ICAT Institute.

What courses does ICAT Institute offer?
ICAT offers 100+ courses including Computer Fundamentals, DCA, ADCA, Tally Prime with GST, Graphic Design, Web Development, Digital Marketing, Programming (C, C++, Java, Python), CCC, Spoken English, Data Entry, and Advanced IT programs like Cyber Security, Cloud Computing, and AI Basics.
Are ICAT certifications recognized by the government?
Yes. ICAT is an ISO 9001:2015 certified institution with state and central government recognition. Our certificates are QR-code verified, digitally signed, and accepted by employers and universities across India. We also have NSDC alignment for skill-based programs.
How do I apply for admission to ICAT?
You can apply by visiting the nearest ICAT franchise center, calling our helpline, or filling the online admission form on our website. Our counselors will guide you through course selection, fee structure, and batch schedules. Walk-in admissions are also available.
Does ICAT provide placement assistance?
Yes, ICAT has a dedicated placement cell with 500+ hiring partners across industries. We provide resume building, interview preparation, soft skills training, and direct placement drives. Our placement rate is 95% for eligible students. We also support entrepreneurship and freelancing.
What is the fee structure for ICAT courses?
Course fees vary by program and center location. ICAT maintains affordable pricing to make quality education accessible to all. Scholarships of up to 50% are available for meritorious students. EMI options and installment plans are available at most centers. Contact your nearest center for specific pricing.
How can I open an ICAT Franchise center?
To start an ICAT franchise, fill our franchise enquiry form or contact our helpline. Requirements include a minimum 200 sq.ft. space, 5+ computers, and basic infrastructure. We provide complete training, branding, curriculum, software, and marketing support. Investment starts from ₹50,000 depending on location and scale.
Does ICAT offer online classes?
Yes, ICAT offers both offline (center-based) and online/hybrid learning options. Our online platform provides recorded lectures, live sessions, practice tests, and digital study material. Students can access learning resources 24/7 from anywhere via our LMS portal.
What is the duration of ICAT courses?
Course durations vary: short-term (1–3 months) like CCC, DEO, Computer Fundamentals; medium-term (3–6 months) like Tally, DCA, Graphic Design, Digital Marketing; long-term (6–12 months) like ADCA, Web Development, Programming. Schedules include morning, afternoon, and evening batches.
